Platform Migration

Move platforms.
Keep everything.

A platform migration is one of the highest-risk operations an ecommerce business can run. When it goes wrong, you lose data, customers, and revenue. We make sure it goes right.

Why migrations go wrong
Products disappear. Customer records corrupt. URLs break with no redirects. These are not rare edge cases. They are what happens when migrations are rushed or mishandled.
How we prevent every one of those problems
We run a full pre-migration audit before a single file moves. We document every URL, verify every product record, and build a complete redirect map before anything touches your live store.
Everything comes with you
Products, variants, inventory levels, customer accounts, order history, and reviews are all transferred with full integrity checks. We do not consider it done until every record is verified on the new platform.
Launch with confidence
Go-live is coordinated to minimize downtime. We monitor the new store closely in the days and weeks after launch, ready to catch and resolve anything that comes up.
